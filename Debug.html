Pinggography


Hello, my name is Clemens, also known as Pinggography, and I am a Hobby photographer. I also post some of my pictures on Instagram and almost every good one on my Website.

I mostly shoot on Sony, but I'm open to any brand! My plans for the future as a photographer are to publish stock photos on various platforms and to be an event photographer.

I mostly photograph planes, cars and landscapes but I also like to take some casual vacations photos.

In my free time I like to read, code and play Minecraft with my friends! I am also part of a Robotics Team where I do some marketing, B2B, 3d modelling and programming.
