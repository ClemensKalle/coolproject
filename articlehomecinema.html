For who is a home cinema worth it?

Home Cinemas are not for everyone and are also often pricey if you aren't chronically on Facebook Marketplace or Kleinanzeigen.
But price isn't everything; there's a saying: no matter the sale, if you don't or can't use it, it's still money thrown out the window.

So, what is a home cinema?
It's for those who care about good sound quality for their high-quality TV.
For the ones who enjoy listening to music like it's a wine tasting
And for those who just care about a good audio system

What kind of home cinemas are out there?
If we are speaking numbers, there are multiple options:

- 2.0 - The first number is the number of channels you have; here it's 2: one left, one right
- 2.1 - The second number is the number of subwoofers, which are basically speakers but for more bass
- 3.1 - Left, Right, Centre
- 5.1 - This adds a left and right surround, which are placed next to you
- 7.1 - This adds another pair of left and right; these are behind you
- 7.1.2 - The third number is how many speakers you got on the ceiling, so you got 2 over your head

There are more like 9.1.2 or 9.2.2, but for a normal user that's over the top; most receivers only support 5.2 or 7.2 anyway, with some offering a second zone.
