Step 2: Testing out colours

After getting a rough image in mind I would recomment using one of those colour palete testers on your wall to see if it fits the mood and then hold it against the furniture you are planning to buy
If you are happy with everything its now time to paint (if you choose to) and if the paint job is finished you can move in your furniture!
