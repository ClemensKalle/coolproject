Step 1: Getting an Image of what you want

Hello everyone and welcome to my first Blog post about my litte home makeover project,
the reason for this is that I am changing rooms and now I have much more space for decorating!
And I am gonna report and write a little bit about my journey with Images and Text.

As inspiration I can always recomment searching on Pinterest,
but I would recomment turning on the "less AI" button so you have more real images and not AI slop.

Here are some examples:


I am personally aiming for RAL 6037, or Pure Green with a White Alex Drawer Table with a darker Brown Wooden Tabletop,
but you can of course choose differently! But I would recomment a Solid wood top if you are not on a budget and if you own a pc.
if you buy one of the cheap Ikea tabletops it will eventually look like a bracket ) , we often call that smiling at you.

Me personally, I got to start by removing the old sofa and replacing it by a newer one but if you dont have to do that you can skip that. XD
After that I have to start filling all the holes in the wall the former resident left there, I would recommend finding a match to the current colour
and painting over the old one, do the whole wall or else it wont look as good, you can only fix the dirty places if you are painting over it with another colour later on.
